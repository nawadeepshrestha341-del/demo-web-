OUR STORY

Every Piece Carries a Legacy

CHAPTER ONE

The Origin, 1978

Amrapali began in Jaipur in 1978, rooted in centuries of Indian craftsmanship. From a single atelier, our founders set out to preserve heritage techniques — kundan, polki, and meenakari — that were at risk of being lost to time.

CHAPTER TWO

The Craft

Every piece passes through the hands of master artisans, many from families who have practiced their craft for generations. Kundan setting, hand-engraving, and stone-carving are done entirely by hand — no shortcuts, no machine casting of the final details.

CHAPTER THREE

Today, Around the World

What began in a single Jaipur atelier now reaches boutiques across India and abroad, carrying that same heritage craftsmanship to a global audience.
